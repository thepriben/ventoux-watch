Pipeline

  1. Frame1 frame/s. ffmpeg reads the HLS stream.
  2. MotionOpenCV MOG2, frame scaled to 640 px. If more than 35% changes, it is light, and it is ignored. The red beacon on the summit is excluded.
  3. TrackA compact blob, tracked for at least 3 frames.
  4. ClassYOLO11 nano, ONNX, on the crop only. Person, car, bus, truck.
  5. PlaneThe sky does not use the model’s airplane class. OpenSky. A callsign is written only when there is one aircraft, or one much lower than the others.
  6. BusTrans'CoVe or ZOU. A single trip within ±15 min gives the route. Otherwise “Bus”, from a confidence of 0.6.
  7. Crowd4 people or more, held for 8 seconds.
  8. FireOn the slope, 20 s, area ×1.5, at least 8% warm pixels. Not at dusk. In rain, fog, or snow, it takes 20%.
  9. RestThe photo is kept, with no invented name. The same spot eight times: a habit. Then at most one card every 6 h.
